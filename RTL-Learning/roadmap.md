# 6-Month RTL Learning Roadmap

## Month 1 — Basics

    - Gates, operators, data types (wire/reg/logic)
    - Blocking vs non-blocking assignments
    - Simple combinational modules (mux, decoder, adder)

## Month 2 — Combinational + Sequential

    - Always blocks, latches vs flip-flops
    - Counters, shift registers, registers with enable/reset

## Month 3 — FSMs

    - Moore vs Mealy machines
    - Traffic light, vending machine, UART FSM examples

## Month 4 — Memory

    - RAM/ROM modeling, FIFOs, dual-port memory

## Month 5 — Protocols

    - SPI, I2C, UART implementations
    - AXI-lite basics

## Month 6 — Projects + Interview Prep

    - End-to-end mini projects (e.g., simple CPU, DMA controller)
    - Timing/verification concepts, interview questions
